CARVEFEST AT AGROFEST 2026

February 27 – March 1, 2026 Location: Queen’s Park Gallery

Three days of live carving demonstrations, learning, exhibition of decorative and utilitarian products, buying, and celebration — all within AGROFEST 2026. CARVEFEST blends spectacle, skill, and commerce into one national experience.

DAY 1 – Schools & Youth
  • Exhibition and Marketplace Open
  • Students Welcome
  • Live Carving and Power Tools Showcase
  • Raffles and Prizes to be Won
DAYS 2 & 3 – Family & Community
  • Exhibition and Marketplace Continue
  • Carving in Motion Demonstrations
  • Artwork and Tools Raffles
  • Talks on Craftsmanship, Entrepreneurship, and Materials
