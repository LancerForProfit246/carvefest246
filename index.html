CARVEFEST 2026 — Embracing Culture - Sustainable Industry

A national woodcarving activation turning heritage into jobs, skills, business growth, and ready-to-buy customers.

CARVEFEST 2026 brings together artisans, master carvers, young apprentices, buyers, producers and sponsors’ brands inside AGROFEST, Barbados. It is where tradition meets opportunity — live carving demonstrations with chainsaw and power tools; youth training opportunities; discussions and making meaningful partnerships that help turn craft into lasting livelihoods; whether for your interest hobby, recreational or professional.

What you will experience:

  • Watch master carvers shape mahogany in real time
  • See high-energy chainsaw carving and large-scale sculptures
  • Discover one-of-a-kind Caribbean pieces you can buy and take home
  • Meet artisans and hear the stories behind their work
  • Perfect for visitors looking to take home a piece of Barbados’s mahogany.
