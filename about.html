Who We Are

CARVEFEST is a collective of woodcarvers showcasing talent, connecting with buyers, and building a sustainable industry.

Our Goal

To make woodcarving a viable, sustainable industry that preserves traditions, drives business, and creates jobs – making it an enterprise that's more than just art.

Our Focus

  • Promote Barbados' woodcarving tradition and heritage
  • Create economic opportunities for artisans
  • Develop a structured, income-producing micro-industry
  • Train the next generation of carvers
  • Showcase the potential of Barbados' exotic timbers

Our Approach

  • Collaboration with local and regional government and businesses, education and community organisations and the crafting sector
  • Host live events and exhibitions (like CARVEFEST)
  • Attract investment and sponsorship
  • Develop skills and pipelines for youth
